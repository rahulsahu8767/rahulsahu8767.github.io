Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Momo Shorts Bot ("the app") is a personal automation tool used only by the owner of the "Momo the Red Panda" channel.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube: uploads videos to the owner's own channel, manages its playlists, and reads the channel's analytics.
  • Google Drive: reads and writes video, image and music files in the owner's own Drive.
  • Instagram / Facebook: publishes the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to create, store and publish the owner's videos and to measure how they perform. Access tokens are kept in encrypted secret storage and are never shared. The app does not sell, share or transfer user data to anyone and does not use it for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Google access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app keeps no user data outside the owner's own Google Drive and accounts; deleting those files removes all data.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
